# R34 — Important data transformations are visualised

**Requirement:** Suitable plots/tables show raw behaviour, constructed surge labels and/or engineered signals.
**Priority:** MUST
**Assessed:** 2026-09-05
**Verdict:** **PASS**

R34 asks for suitable plots/tables covering the *data-transformation* side of the pipeline:
raw behaviour, the constructed surge labels, and/or engineered signals ("and/or" — not all
three are strictly required). The report covers **all three** with a mix of figures and
tables, every referenced figure exists as a file under `reports/05-final/figures/`, and the
figures are produced by code (EDA notebooks + `evaluation_figures.py`) so they are
reproducible rather than pasted in.

---

## 1. Raw behaviour — visualised ✔

**Tables**
- `@tbl:eda-highlevel` — per-dataset schema, date span, engagement fields, surge-label
  readiness (raw dataset profiling).
- `@tbl:eda-deep` — records assessed, temporal coverage / gaps, VADER-vs-TextBlob sentiment
  agreement, viable surge definitions, best positive-class rate.
- `@tbl:loader-attrition` — raw records loaded → excluded (no tickers) → exploded pairs.
- `@tbl:dataset-characteristics` — raw records, date range, exploded/usable counts, train/
  test split, test surges, imbalance ratio (the end-to-end raw→modelling transformation).

**Figures (files present)**
- `@fig:eda-cross-dataset` → `figures/fig6-eda-cross-dataset-comparison.png` — the two
  communities side by side on volume, coverage and signal properties.
- A missingness figure also exists: `figures/fig7-eda-missingness-wsb.png` (raw
  per-column missingness), generated from `src/eda/output/figures/missingness_*.png`.

## 2. Constructed surge labels — visualised ✔

- `@fig:eda-viability` → `figures/fig5-eda-surge-viability-wsb.png` — the surge-viability
  sweep: each cell is the **positive-class (surge-label) rate** for a candidate surge
  definition (volume percentile × standard-deviation multiplier). This is a direct
  visualisation of the constructed label under varying definitions. A pennystocks
  counterpart file also exists (`figures/fig5b-eda-surge-viability-pennystocks.png`).
- `@tbl:eda-deep` — "Viable surge definitions (4/9, 3/9)" and "Best positive-class rate
  (5.8%, 5.0%)" tabulate the label construction at screening time.
- `@tbl:dataset-characteristics` — "Test surges (31 / 668)" and "Test imbalance ratio
  (105:1 / 102:1)" show the final constructed labels.
- `@tbl:weight-sensitivity` — surge *rate* (0.53% → 9.62%) as the composite weight $w_2$
  varies, i.e. how the label transformation responds to the target definition.

## 3. Engineered signals — visualised ✔

- `@tbl:feature-definitions` and `@tbl:feature-detail` — the eleven engineered features with
  category, definition, and per-feature computation method (the feature-engineering
  transformation, in tabular form).
- `@fig:feature-importance` → `figures/feature_importance_comparison.png` — permutation
  importance of the engineered signals across both models and both datasets, visualising
  which engineered signals carry information.
- `@tbl:feature-importance` — top-5 permutation importances per model/dataset.

## Verdict

**R34: PASS.** The report visualises all three transformation aspects the criterion names:
raw behaviour (`@tbl:eda-highlevel`, `@tbl:eda-deep`, `@tbl:loader-attrition`,
`@tbl:dataset-characteristics`, `@fig:eda-cross-dataset`, plus a missingness figure),
constructed surge labels (`@fig:eda-viability` positive-class-rate sweep, `@tbl:eda-deep`,
`@tbl:dataset-characteristics` surge counts, `@tbl:weight-sensitivity`), and engineered
signals (`@tbl:feature-definitions`, `@tbl:feature-detail`, `@fig:feature-importance`,
`@tbl:feature-importance`). The referenced figures exist as files and are produced by the
EDA notebooks and `evaluation_figures.py`, so they are suitable and reproducible. The
"and/or" bar is comfortably exceeded.

### Minor observations (not blocking)

- The clearest single "raw time series → constructed surge" visual is the EDA
  surge-viability sweep (`@fig:eda-viability`), which is a screening-phase heuristic
  distinct from the pipeline's composite z-score label; the report states this boundary
  explicitly ([@sec:eda-tooling]). A raw per-ticker volume time series with the labelled
  surge windows overlaid would be an even more direct illustration, but R34 is already
  satisfied by the existing plots/tables — this is a possible enhancement, not a gap.
- Several transformation views are tables rather than plots; R34 explicitly permits
  "plots/tables", so this is fully compliant.
