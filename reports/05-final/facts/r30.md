# R30 — Limitations are evidence-based

**Requirement:** Data, labels, sampling, sentiment, generalisation, temporal scope and
modelling limitations relevant to actual experiments are discussed.
**Priority:** MUST
**Assessed:** 2026-09-05
**Verdict:** **PASS**

R30 is a report-quality requirement: the report must discuss limitations across the seven
named dimensions — **data, labels, sampling, sentiment, generalisation, temporal scope, and
modelling** — and each must relate to the *actual* experiments performed rather than being
generic caveats. All seven dimensions are covered, primarily in the *Limitations*
subsection ([`### Limitations`]) and reinforced by the *Critical Analysis* and *Conclusion →
Limitations and Future Work* sections. Every quantitative claim the limitations rest on
traces to a real pipeline artefact in `output/`, and the two numbers the discussion leans
on most (the 5-seed AUC spread and the Phase-1 volume-only control) were re-verified against
the experiment log below.

## Verification of the numbers the limitations rely on

The limitations discussion is only "evidence-based" if its supporting figures are genuine
pipeline outputs. The two load-bearing quantitative claims were checked against
`output/experiment_log.jsonl` and `output/evaluation/`:

### (a) Training-variance claim — "0.019 AUC range across 5 seeds"

Limitations text: *"training variance only partially characterised (0.019 AUC range across
5 seeds)"*; Implementation Status: *"five seeds (42, 123, 456, 789, 2024) … AUC spanning
0.734 to 0.753 (a 0.019 margin)"*.

Verified from the experiment log (pennystocks, τ = 1.5, w₁ = w₂ = 0.5):

| seed | best model | best AUC-ROC |
|------|------------|--------------|
| 42   | random_forest | 0.752674925737902 |
| 123  | xgboost       | 0.7337343652205014 |
| 456  | xgboost       | 0.7337343652205014 |
| 789  | random_forest | 0.7425216328720308 |
| 2024 | random_forest | 0.746152776259972 |

min = 0.7337, max = 0.7527, **margin = 0.0189** → rounds to "0.734 to 0.753 (0.019)". ✔

### (b) Label-circularity control — "with w₂ = 0, XGBoost still achieves AUC 0.710"

Limitations text (Structural correlation): *"Phase 1 provides a partial control: with
w₂ = 0, XGBoost still achieves AUC 0.710."*

Verified: the volume-only (w_sentiment = 0) training runs recorded in the log yield XGBoost
AUC **0.7102** at τ = 1.5 (run `2026-07-19_09-28`) and **0.7096** at τ = 1.0
(run `2026-07-16_18-52`), consistent with the Phase-1 value 0.710 in
`@tbl:phase-comparison`. ✔

### (c) Sample-size claim — "31 test surges"

Verified: `support_positive = 31`, `support_negative = 3247`, `n_test_samples = 3278`
appear across the pennystocks evaluation artefacts in `output/evaluation/` (e.g.
`2026-07-19_13-28_evaluation_metrics.json`), matching `@tbl:dataset-characteristics`. The
"bootstrap CIs spanning ±0.08–0.09" claim is consistent with the CIs reported in
`@tbl:perf-default` (RF 0.753 [0.673–0.824]). ✔

## Quality of the discussion (beyond mere presence)

The limitations are not boilerplate: several are self-critical in ways only possible from
having run the experiments.

- **Honest label critique.** The report flags that its dominant feature (`sentiment_score`)
  is structurally correlated with the composite target, explicitly calls this "a
  circularity that likely inflates sentiment's apparent importance", and supplies the
  Phase-1 control rather than dismissing the concern.
- **Distinguishes failure modes with evidence.** §Temporal Stability separates *ranking*
  stability (AUC 0.880 holds on test) from *threshold* collapse (val-F1 0.911 → test-F1
  0.210), concluding the instability is calibration, not overfitting — a claim grounded in
  the observed AUC/F1 divergence, not assertion.
- **Ties limitations to design choices.** Error analysis attributes cold-start false
  negatives to the deliberate exclusion of network/external-news signals ([@tbl:signal-families])
  and false positives partly to ticker-extraction noise (Assumption 3), linking limitations
  back to documented trade-offs ([@tbl:design-alternatives]).
- **Bounds the deployment claim.** The class-imbalance limitation states a precision ceiling
  holds even for a perfect ranker at sub-1% prevalence, motivating rank-based deployment —
  consistent with the operational-precision experiment ([@tbl:acceptance-eval]).

## Verdict

**R30: PASS.** The report discusses limitations across all seven required dimensions
(data, labels, sampling, sentiment, generalisation, temporal scope, modelling), and each is
tied to an experiment that was actually performed rather than stated generically. The
supporting figures the limitations depend on — the 0.019 five-seed AUC spread, the
Phase-1 volume-only control AUC of 0.710, and the 31 pennystocks test surges — were
re-verified against `output/experiment_log.jsonl` and `output/evaluation/` and match the
report. The discussion is additionally self-critical (label circularity, ranking-vs-
threshold distinction, imbalance ceiling), meeting the intent of the criterion rather than
only its letter.

(No status change was required elsewhere; this finding is consistent with R24, R25, R26 and
R27, which cover the class-imbalance, error, robustness and results analyses the limitations
draw on.)
