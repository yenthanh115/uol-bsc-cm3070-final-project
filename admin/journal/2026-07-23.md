## Scope statement revision — dual-dataset rationale articulated

### Context

The report's §1.4 Scope previously listed the two datasets (r/pennystocks: 80,212 records; r/wallstreetbets: 1,293,981 records) without explaining *why* those two were chosen or what the dual-dataset design is intended to test.

### Change made to `reports/04-draft/draft-report-v0.3.md`

Expanded the in-scope statement to explicitly frame the two subreddits as opposite ends of a "data density spectrum":
- r/pennystocks as a sparse, niche community
- r/wallstreetbets as a high-volume mainstream forum

Added the experimental motivation: this dual-dataset design tests whether the methodology generalises across different community sizes and posting behaviours, or whether data density is a binding constraint on model performance.

### Rationale

This is an articulation of the design reasoning behind DEC-005 (Dataset selection), not a new decision. The datasets were already chosen; the edit formalises the *experimental contrast* they represent. This framing strengthens the report's justification for using two datasets rather than one — it's not just "generalisability testing" (as DEC-005 states) but a specific hypothesis about data density as a variable.
